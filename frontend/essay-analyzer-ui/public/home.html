How it works

Check handwriting in 4 simple steps

No manual typing. No switching apps. Everything happens inside Google Classroom.

1

Click Check Spelling

A button appears directly in your Google Classroom page. Click it to open the spell checker panel.

2

Select a student

Choose your course, assignment, and student. Their handwriting images load automatically from Drive.

3

AI checks the work

Claude AI reads the handwriting and identifies spelling and grammar errors in seconds.

4

Review and download

Accept or reject each correction, add teacher notes, and download a printable report card.
